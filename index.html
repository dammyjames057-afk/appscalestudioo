FAQ

Questions buyers
usually ask.

Do you work with Android and iOS apps?

Yes. Campaigns can be planned around Android, iOS, mobile games and other app products depending on the project requirements.

Can you promote mobile games?

Yes. Game apps can be supported with audience research, promotional strategy, advertising and digital visibility activities.

Do you provide Google Ads campaign management?

Yes. Support can include campaign setup, audience targeting, monitoring, optimization and performance tracking.

Do you provide ASO?

Yes. ASO support can include keyword research, competitor research and app-store listing strategy.

Do you guarantee installs or rankings?

No. App performance depends on the product, market, budget, competition, store listing and advertising environment. The focus is on sound strategy and optimization.

Do I need an advertising budget?

For paid advertising, yes. Advertising spend is separate from the marketing service fee and remains under the client's control.
